    Expected rates are for the homes in each map not yet knocked since 1 June 2026 (homes in buildings reported as no access or letterbox only are left out). They come from each household's age mix, size, gender mix, ancestry (inferred from names), unit/house and any 2022 conversation, from a model fitted on every Northern Metro district's knocks with the effect of individual volunteers removed, then blended with each suburb's actual Brunswick results so far in proportion to doors answered (points of support: Brunswick +1.2, Brunswick East −0.2, Brunswick West +0.5, Fitzroy North −0.9, Carlton North −1.9, Princes Hill −2.1). One result per enrolled dwelling, each unit counted. Maps crossing a suburb boundary sit in the suburb most of their enrolled homes are in. Maps with fewer than 10 enrolled homes are greyed out. The default ranking is the expected score per answered door: supporters, plus each neutral answer (a 3 or busy) counted as part of a supporter in proportion to the household's lean (support ÷ (support + opposition)), averaging a quarter of a supporter. A map is finished when 95% or more of its enrolled homes have been knocked since 1 June 2026, or fewer than 5 are left (as on the progress map).
